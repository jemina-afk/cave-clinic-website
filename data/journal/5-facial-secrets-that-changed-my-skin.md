After attending a professional Facials & Skin Care Masterclass, I discovered techniques that completely transformed the way I approach treatments. These are game-changing secrets you should know before your next facial.

## 1. Clear, unclogged skin

Pre-extraction gel shifts blackhead acidity to an alkaline level (pH 7), making them release more easily. The result? Skin that feels clear, fresh and congestion-free.

## 2. Protected, blurred skin

Spritzing toner before applying moisturiser or SPF helps close pores, protecting your skin barrier. It also creates a smooth, blurred finish that makes skin look flawless.

## 3. Anti-ageing boost

Pure vitamins A, C and E absorb instantly without clogging pores. These nutrients fight signs of ageing while giving your skin the glow it craves.

## 4. Special care for ethnic skin

Black and Asian skin types need extra attention. They heal more slowly and are more prone to dark spots and hyperpigmentation from injury. Gentle, tailored care is essential to prevent long-term marks.

## 5. Fine line eraser

Consistent treatments with muscle manipulation techniques can smooth out wrinkles by breaking down the fibres that cause creasing. Over time, this visibly reduces fine lines.

Your skin deserves more than just a basic cleanse.
