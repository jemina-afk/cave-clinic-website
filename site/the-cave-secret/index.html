In short

One face, planned as a whole.

We look at your natural structure, symmetry and proportions. We use the golden ratio as a guide, not a rule. Instead of isolating one feature, we consider how each area works together. So no one can tell what's been done, only that you look refreshed.

    How it works

    Four quiet steps.

    1. Consultation

      By video or in person. We start with what's bothering you, and why.

    2. Your facial map

      We assess structure, symmetry and proportion, and agree up to 3 areas.

    3. Treatment

      Paris treats each area as part of one plan, never in isolation.

    4. Review

      We check in once everything has settled, and fine-tune if needed.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Who is facial balancing for?

    It's for anyone who wants a refreshed, balanced look without obvious change. We assess your face as a whole first. [Answer to confirm with Paris]

    How many ml do I need?

    Most clients choose Refine, 3ml. Your plan and amount are confirmed at consultation, never before. [Answer to confirm with Paris]

    How long do results last?

    It depends on the treatment and the area. Generally, results last between 6 and 18 months. [Answer to confirm with Paris]

    What happens at the consultation?

    We talk about what's bothering you and why. Then we map your face and agree a plan together. [Answer to confirm with Paris]
