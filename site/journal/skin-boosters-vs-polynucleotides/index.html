Skin Boosters vs Polynucleotides: Which Glow-Up Is Right for You?

If you've ever layered 14 serums, gua sha'd your soul out, and still looked a bit dull… it might be time for an upgrade. Injectable skincare is where the real glow lives.

We help clients from across the UK ditch the guesswork and invest in what their skin actually needs. So what's the difference between skin boosters and polynucleotides, and which one is right for you?

Skin boosters: hydration from the inside out

Skin boosters are injectable treatments designed to flood the skin with moisture, improve elasticity, and leave you looking fresher, dewier and more youthful. Think of it as the injectable version of your best hydrating serum, just with longer-lasting results.

  • Made from hyaluronic acid (HA)
  • Hydrates, plumps and smooths skin texture
  • Perfect for dry, tired or crepey skin
  • Great for face, neck, chest and hands
  • Ideal as a pre-event glow-up or part of a long-term skin plan

Popular options we use at CAVE include Profhilo® and Seventy Hyal, depending on your needs and skin type.

Polynucleotides: regeneration on a cellular level

Polynucleotides are a newer tier in skin regeneration. Originally used in medical wound healing, they're now used in aesthetics to repair skin, improve tone and texture, and support collagen, especially for under-eyes and delicate areas.

  • Made from purified DNA fragments
  • Supports elasticity and calms inflammation
  • Targets dark circles, crepey under-eyes and early signs of ageing
  • Suitable for sensitive or rosacea-prone skin
  • Long-term results with minimal downtime

So… which one should you choose?

It depends on your skin goals.

  • Dull, dehydrated skin: skin boosters
  • Tired or dark under-eyes: polynucleotides
  • Loss of bounce and elasticity: either, or both
  • Early signs of ageing: polynucleotides
  • A quick, radiant glow: skin boosters

Still unsure? That's exactly what our consultations are for. We assess your skin, your lifestyle and your long-term goals to build a personalised plan, sometimes combining both treatments for that glass skin, but make it real effect.
