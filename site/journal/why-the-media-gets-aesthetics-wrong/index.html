Why the Media Gets the Aesthetics Industry All Wrong

Let's talk about the fear-mongering, the double standards, and what's really true.

They criticise us, but they don't understand us. You've probably seen the headlines.

One moment, the media glorifies "perfect" faces and filtered bodies. Next, it tears down anyone who chooses aesthetic treatments.

Here's why we shouldn't listen.

1. Fear-mongering sells

The media tends to spotlight rare adverse events while ignoring the overwhelming number of safe, successful treatments. This creates fear and uncertainty, rather than education.

2. The moral high ground? Hypocritical

Public figures undergo countless treatments behind the scenes, yet the media shames everyday people for enhancing their features. Where's the consistency?

3. Clickbait culture drives misinformation

Dramatic stories grab clicks, but they skew perception and overshadow the real benefits aesthetics can bring to confidence and wellbeing.

4. Expertise is often missing

Most reports lack input from trained professionals. Without context, safety guidelines or scientific nuance, coverage can't be trusted.

5. They don't show the whole picture

The aesthetics industry serves people of all genders, backgrounds and ages. The media rarely reflects this truth.

What we believe at CAVE Clinic

  • Aesthetic treatments are personal decisions.
  • You deserve to look and feel your best, without shame.
  • The media lacks nuance, not us.

Let's take their judgement with a pinch of salt and continue celebrating your choice to feel confident and look your best.
