Ethics · 29 September 2025 · Paris Tyrell

The Truth About Ethics in Aesthetics

There's a fine line between enhancement and exploitation.

Too many practitioners avoid this conversation out of fear of losing clients. But I believe the opposite is true: honesty builds trust.

At CAVE Clinic, here's what I stand for.

1. Your best interests come first

During consultations, I don't suggest "fixes" unless you ask. Instead, I begin with one question: what's bothering you and why?

From there, we focus on solutions that genuinely improve your confidence without making you self-conscious about something you never noticed.

2. Perfection is a myth

We all start with different foundations, and that's what makes us unique. Aesthetics should enhance what you already have, helping you become your best self, not someone else.

3. Protecting you from harm

If I believe a client is struggling with body dysmorphia, I won't treat them. It's unethical to fuel a mindset where no result will ever feel "enough."

Sometimes, the kindest thing I can do is say no.

4. Ethics over profit

The industry is full of people chasing a quick buck. CAVE is about more than treatments. It's about care, trust and confidence-building that lasts.

If this resonates with you and you're ready for aesthetics that put ethics first, let's talk. Because your beauty is personal, and so is the way I protect it.
